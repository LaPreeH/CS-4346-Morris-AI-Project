CS4346 Morris AI Tournament — Improved Static Estimation

1. FEATURES

The improved estimator evaluates the position from White's point of view;
positive scores favor White and negative scores favor Black. The exact same
function supports both colors, since Black searches for the lowest score.

- Piece advantage: number of White pieces minus number of Black pieces.
- Completed mills: difference in the number of three-piece lines.
- Potential mills: difference in lines with two friendly pieces and one vacancy.
- Opening positional flexibility: difference in neighbor counts of occupied
  locations, rewarding pieces at intersections with several possible routes.
- Midgame/endgame mobility: difference in the number of legal successor boards.
- Midgame/endgame blocked pieces: difference in pieces with no adjacent vacancy
  (only counted for a side with more than three pieces).
- Terminal situations: opponent reduced to <=2 pieces or unable to move means
  a win, with a very large positive/negative score.

2. WHY THESE FEATURES

Material advantage is important because the goal is to capture pieces. A
completed mill creates a defensive structure. A two-piece line containing an
empty spot poses a near-term threat of making a mill. Mobility and access to
high-degree locations provide future choices. Blocking matters because a side
can lose when it cannot move. Terminal wins should outrank small positional
advantages.

3. WEIGHTS AND COMBINATION

Opening phase:
100 * (WhitePieces - BlackPieces)
+ 45 * (WhiteMills - BlackMills)
+ 24 * (WhitePotentialMills - BlackPotentialMills)
+ 3 * (sum of White occupied neighbor degrees
       - sum of Black occupied neighbor degrees)

Midgame/endgame:
1000 * (WhitePieces - BlackPieces)
+ 90 * (WhiteMills - BlackMills)
+ 35 * (WhitePotentialMills - BlackPotentialMills)
+ 3 * (WhiteMoves - BlackMoves)
+ 15 * (BlockedBlackPieces - BlockedWhitePieces)

A decisive terminal position returns +100000 if Black cannot continue and
-100000 if White cannot continue. This is a heuristic, not a claim that the
weights are mathematically optimal. For exact implementation details see
improved_evaluation in morris_engine.py.

4. WHY IT MAY PLAY BETTER THAN THE ORIGINAL

The handout's opening estimator is only WhitePieces - BlackPieces, so many
placements at the same search depth tie. The improved estimator separates
moves that establish future mills and useful strategic positions. In the
midgame the original strongly emphasizes material and Black's mobility but
omits White's flexibility, mills, and threats. Our version considers both
players symmetrically and more aspects of short-term tactics. Its advantage
must still be measured in tournament play; stronger performance is not
mathematically guaranteed.

5. TWO REPRODUCIBLE DIFFERENT-MOVE EXAMPLES

Both examples are White to move in the opening at depth 2. Enter each input
board in a text file and run MiniMaxOpening.py and MiniMaxOpeningImproved.py
against that file with the same depth. Each example contains exactly 23 slots.

EXAMPLE 1
Input:           xBxxxBxxxxxxWxBxxxWxxxW
Original move:   WBxxxBxxxxxxWxBxxxWxxxW
Improved move:   xBxxxBxxxxxxWxBxxxWWxxW

The original move is the first tied optimal move in search order. Both leave
4 White and 3 Black pieces, but the improved move leaves White with two
potential mills (two White pieces and an empty slot) instead of none. These
future mill threats are strategically preferable to an arbitrary placement.

EXAMPLE 2
Input:           xxxBxWBBBBxxBxxWWxxWxWW
Original move:   xxxxxWBBBBxxBxWWWxxWxWW
Improved move:   xxxxxWBBBBxxBxxWWxWWxWW

Both successor boards have 7 White and 5 Black pieces and two completed White
mills. The original continuation has two White potential mills; the improved
continuation has three. Thus the new heuristic prefers an additional avenue
for forming a mill and capturing an opponent's piece later.

6. ALPHA-BETA SAVINGS (ORIGINAL ESTIMATOR)

Example A: opening, depth 3
Input: xxBxxxxxxxxWxxBxxBWxxxW
MINIMAX: 5290 static evaluations
ALPHA-BETA: 1943 static evaluations
Both estimate 1 and choose xxBxxxxxxxxWxxBxxBWxWxW.

Example B: opening, depth 3
Input: BBxBBxWxxxxWxBxWWWxxxxx
MINIMAX: 7180 static evaluations
ALPHA-BETA: 584 static evaluations
Both estimate 2 and choose xBxBBxWxxxxWxBWWWWxxxxx.

Notes: The source handout says that if every opponent piece is in a mill, no
piece is removed when a mill is formed. This program follows that rule.
The full tournament move time limit and input protocol were not supplied;
the tournament program uses explicit color and phase arguments, and the
chosen depth should be benchmarked against the actual deadline.
